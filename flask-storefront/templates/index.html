BPC-157 — The Systemic Healing Catalyst & NO Modulator

Activation Pathway:

15-AA gastric peptide→VEGFR2 Internalization→Akt-eNOS Activation→NO Synthesis→Angiogenesis
NO Homeostat

Uniquely balances nitric oxide — raises when low (preventing ischaemia), suppresses when high (preventing oxidative stress).

Inflammation Control

Lowers TNF-α, IL-6, COX-2, MPO — potent anti-inflammatory profile across multiple pathways.

Clinical Translation

Accelerated tendon/ligament healing with organised collagen deposition and no unwanted ossicle formation.

GHK-Cu — The Epigenetic Switchboard for Dermal Regeneration

Modulates ~31% of human genes. Plasma levels decline from ~200 ng/mL at age 20 to ~80 ng/mL at age 60.

↑ Upregulation

Collagen I & III · Decorin · Angiogenesis (VEGF/bFGF) · Antioxidant Defense

↓ Downregulation

Inflammation (NF-κB, p38 MAPK) · TNF-β · IL-6 · Matrix Metalloproteinases

Clinical Translation: Essential cofactor for lysyl oxidase. Accelerates wound closure by 30–50%. Injectable form achieves tissue concentrations 10–20× higher than topicals.

TB-500 — Cytoskeletal Dynamics & The Migration Engine

Molecular definition: Synthetic 7-amino-acid fragment (Ac-LKKTETQ) of full-length Thymosin Beta-4.

Before (Injury Site)

Impaired actin dynamics — cells clustered, unable to migrate to repair site.

Post TB-500

Actin upregulation & cell migration — rapid movement to injury site, re-epithelialisation and muscle/cardiac tissue repair.

Mechanism: Binds actin, transforming the cytoskeleton to enable rapid cell migration. Promotes angiogenesis and vascular regeneration systemically.

Retatrutide — The Triple-Agonist Metabolic Powerhouse

Mechanism: A single 39-amino-acid analogue that simultaneously activates three receptors — the broadest metabolic signalling of any peptide in development.

GLP-1 receptor+GIP receptor+Glucagon receptor→Appetite ↓ · Glucose control · Thermogenesis ↑
GLP-1 + GIP

Suppresses appetite and slows gastric emptying while improving insulin sensitivity and glucose handling.

Glucagon Arm

The distinguishing third axis — raises energy expenditure and drives hepatic fat mobilisation (thermogenic effect).

Clinical Translation

Phase 2 trials reported up to ~24% mean body-weight reduction at 48 weeks — among the largest seen for a metabolic agent.

Investigational — not FDA-approved. For research & educational purposes only.

Epitalon & Semax — Neuroprotection & Telomere Biology
Epitalon — The Cellular Clock

Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly tetrapeptide. Activates telomerase (hTERT), modulates pineal gland / melatonin synthesis. 12–24% lifespan extension in rodent models.

Semax — Neuroplasticity

ACTH(4-10) analogue. Produces 3× increase in BDNF mRNA expression and TrkB activation. Supports cognitive enhancement and stroke recovery. Widely used in Eastern clinical practice.
